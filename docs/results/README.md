## End-to-end time

11 full evaluations (5 photos, about 222 KB per check): **4.5 s median** from tapping Analyze to the report being ready (mean 4.7 s, slowest 5.7 s); the upload itself took 0.37 s on the local network. Backend and ML service on one laptop CPU (no GPU). Reports were written by the rule template (no LLM key yet); the LLM breed check and AI-written report will add time once the key is set; not measured yet (run this script again then). On mobile data the upload takes longer; at 1 Mbit/s, 222 KB is about 1.8 s.

## Changes made from these measurements (Module 9)

Tuned only on separate data (the breed project's validation split and Animals-10 photos not used in the tests above), then measured once on the test sets.

- **Sheep rule in the cattle gate**: sheep accepted as cattle 25/60 → 8/60; all not-cattle rejected 89.6 % → 94.1 %; cattle accepted 94.8 % → 94.5 %.
- **Breed: species filter only when the species step is at least 75 % sure.** Always filtering cost accuracy because the species step is wrong more often than the breed model on this set. Breed accuracy 64.9 % → 70.6 %, macro-F1 0.637 → 0.713, top-3 85.7 % → 92.9 %. When a breed of the other species wins, it is marked uncertain (shown as "possibly …").
- **Teeth photo not used for health** (Module 7): on the 449 holdout teeth photos of healthy animals the disease model said foot-and-mouth for 42 % of them.

## Data and caveats

- **Breed / species**: the team's 15-breed dataset, held-out *test* split (cluster-aware, no leakage per its DATASET_SOURCE.md). Cow:buffalo is 12:1 here, which is why buffalo precision looks low; Pakistani mandis have far more buffaloes. On genuinely different photos (Hugging Face `SynthAIzer/indian-cattle-buffalo-breeds`) breed accuracy is much lower (about 20–25 % on breeds the model knows): the LLM breed check from a side photo is the main safeguard.
- **Not-cattle**: Animals-10 (Hugging Face `Rapidata/Animals-10`: cat, chicken, dog, elephant, horse, sheep) + 37 Wikimedia Commons photos. Horses are the hardest case (11/60 accepted): brown cattle share ImageNet's "sorrel" class.
- **Age**: the age project's holdout fold (never used for training).
- **Health**: five public GitHub sets (`11Adars`, `tleeds1`, `Abdo965`, `SuryaGudipati21`, `shubh-07-lk`). They may overlap the Hugging Face model's training data, so these numbers are likely optimistic; real mandi photos are needed (field test).
- **Health false alarms on spotted coats**: Rathi cows with dense white-spotted coats were called lumpy skin in 6 of 7 breed-dataset photos (scores 0.6–0.8; found while building `demo_images/`). Spotted breeds need a vet's look before trusting a lumpy-skin flag.
- No GPU; times are single-photo calls after warm-up.
